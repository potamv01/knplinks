Patient self-check tools

Free, structured checklists built on published diagnostic criteria. They help you describe a problem accurately to your GP, and help clinicians route it to the right place first time. None of them diagnose anything.

Midlife ear and balance checklist

For adults roughly 40 to 65 with blocked or full ears, an echoing voice, crackling, dizziness or unsteadiness. Separates the two Eustachian tube patterns from vestibular causes, flags what needs urgent assessment, and covers when blood tests are worth doing.

Clinician and patient views · printable · 5 minutes

These tools are screening aids, not diagnostic tests, and do not replace clinical examination. If you have sudden hearing loss, continuous vertigo with new neurological symptoms, or any other symptom marked urgent within a tool, contact your GP practice or emergency services rather than waiting for an appointment.

No supplement, device or branded product is recommended anywhere in this collection. Free to print, share and use in practice.
